---
song: Once in a Lifetime
artist: Talking Heads
album: Remain in Light
reading: A Doll's House — Henrik Ibsen
---

The whole song is a man waking up inside his own life and not recognizing it. "How did I get here?" — the beautiful house, the beautiful wife — all of it suddenly strange, as if he sleepwalked into a life he never quite chose. Byrne delivers it like a preacher mid-breakdown, "letting the days go by" while the water keeps moving underneath.

That's Nora's exact moment in A Doll's House: the comfortable home revealed, all at once, as a role she had been playing rather than a life she had been living. Both are about the vertigo of seeing your ordinary existence from the outside.

The real question is what comes after the question. Once you have asked "how did I get here," can you keep living the same way? Nora walks out the door. Does the man in the song — or does he let the days go by?
