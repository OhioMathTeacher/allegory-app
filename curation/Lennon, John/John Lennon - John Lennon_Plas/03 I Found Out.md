---
song: I Found Out
artist: John Lennon
album: Plastic Ono Band
reading: The Allegory of the Cave — Plato
---

The title is the whole move: *I found out*. This is the freed prisoner in Plato's cave — the one who turns from the shadows and sees them for what they are. The illusions Lennon names (religion, gurus, dope) are the cave's shadows; "feel your own pain" is the ache of the eyes adjusting to real light.

The angle: liberation as disenchantment. But press the edge — Plato's prisoner climbs *toward* a higher truth, the sun; Lennon climbs *out* toward nothing higher, just self-reliance. Worth asking the listener: when you "find out," do you find a truer reality, or only the absence of the lies?
